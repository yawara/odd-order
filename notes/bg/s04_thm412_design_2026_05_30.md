### LEAF-1 `isCyclic_commutator_of_isMetacyclic` (a-2 sub-piece) — target `IsMetacyclic.lean`
```lean
namespace OddOrder.GroupTheory.IsMetacyclic
/-- A metacyclic group has cyclic commutator subgroup: `R' ⊆ N` (the cyclic normal piece,
since `R ⧸ N` is abelian) and subgroups of a cyclic group are cyclic. -/
theorem isCyclic_commutator {G : Type*} [Group G] (hmeta : OddOrder.GroupTheory.IsMetacyclic G) :
    IsCyclic (commutator G) := by
  obtain ⟨N, hN, hN_cyc, hQ_cyc⟩ := hmeta
  haveI := hN
  haveI : IsCyclic (G ⧸ N) := hQ_cyc
  have hle : commutator G ≤ N :=
    Subgroup.Normal.quotient_commutative_iff_commutator_le.mp inferInstance
  haveI : IsCyclic N := hN_cyc
  haveI : IsCyclic ↥((commutator G).subgroupOf N) := Subgroup.isCyclic _
  exact isCyclic_of_surjective (Subgroup.subgroupOfEquivOfLe hle).toMonoidHom
    (Subgroup.subgroupOfEquivOfLe hle).surjective
end OddOrder.GroupTheory.IsMetacyclic
```
deps: `Subgroup.Normal.quotient_commutative_iff_commutator_le` (mathlib), `Subgroup.isCyclic`,
`Subgroup.subgroupOfEquivOfLe`, `IsCyclic.commGroup` instance. tractability = **easy**。

### LEAF-4 `isCyclic_of_card_omega1_le_prime` (a-3 + Prop 4.11 sub-piece) — target `S04`
```lean
-- namespace OddOrder.BG.Ch1.S04, variable {R} [Group R] [Finite R] {p} [Fact p.Prime]
/-- **Converse of BG Lemma 4.5**: a finite `p`-group (`p` odd) with `|Ω₁(R)| ≤ p` is cyclic.
Every order-`p` subgroup lies in `Ω₁(R)`; if `|Ω₁(R)| ≤ p` it is the unique such subgroup,
so `R` is cyclic by `isCyclic_of_subgroups_card_prime_unique_of_odd`. -/
theorem isCyclic_of_card_omega1_le_prime (hR : IsPGroup p R) (hp_odd : Odd p)
    (hΩ : Nat.card (Omega R p 1) ≤ p) : IsCyclic R := by
  apply OddOrder.Isaacs.Ch06.isCyclic_of_subgroups_card_prime_unique_of_odd hR hp_odd
  intro K L hK hL
  have memle : ∀ M : Subgroup R, Nat.card M = p → M ≤ Omega R p 1 := by
    intro M hM g hg
    have hgp : g ^ p = 1 := by
      have h1 : (⟨g, hg⟩ : M) ^ Nat.card M = 1 := pow_card_eq_one'
      have := congrArg Subtype.val h1
      rwa [SubmonoidClass.coe_pow, OneMemClass.coe_one, hM] at this
    exact Omega.mem_of_pow_eq_one (by rw [pow_one]; exact hgp)
  rw [Subgroup.eq_of_le_of_card_ge (memle K hK) (by rw [hK]; exact hΩ),
      Subgroup.eq_of_le_of_card_ge (memle L hL) (by rw [hL]; exact hΩ)]
```

## 6. mmd 原典転記 (L1588-1622, 検証用)

```
Theorem 4.12 (Huppert): p odd prime, R metacyclic p-group, A a p′-group of operators on R. Then
(a) [induction on |R|] By Prop 1.6(b), [R,A]=[R,A,A]. Therefore assume R=[R,A].
    R metacyclic ⇒ R′ cyclic. Take cyclic A-invariant S maximal s.t. R′⊆S. Then S⊴R and S⊴RA.
    S cyclic ⇒ Aut S abelian. Therefore by (4.8), R=[R,A]⊆(RA)′⊆C_{RA}(S). Thus S⊆Z(R).
    R′⊆S ⇒ R/S abelian. Regard Ω₁(R/S) as F_p-vector space and A-module. By Maschke, ∃ A-invariant
    complement X/S to A-submodule Ω₁(R)S/S of Ω₁(R/S). By Lem 4.10, Ω₁(R) elem ab. Thus
    X∩Ω₁(R)=X∩Ω₁(R)∩S=Ω₁(S). Hence |Ω₁(X)|≤|Ω₁(S)|=p. By Lem 4.5, X cyclic. By maximal choice of S,
    X=S. Thus Ω₁(R/S)=Ω₁(R)S/S. By Lem 4.10, |Ω₁(R)|≤p², so |Ω₁(R/S)|≤p. By Lem 4.5, R/S cyclic.
    Thus R/Z(R) cyclic, hence R abelian (Lem 4.1).
(b) T=[R,A]. By (a) and Prop 1.6, [T,A]=T, R=TC_R(A), T=[T,A]×C_T(A)=T×(T∩C_R(A)). Hence T∩C_R(A)=1.
(c) T=[R,A]. By (a), 1⊂T⊂R, so C_R(A)≠1. By (b), T∩C_R(A)=1, therefore
    |Ω₁(R)|≥|Ω₁(T)||Ω₁(C_R(A))|≥p². By Lem 4.10, |Ω₁(R)|≤p². Thus |Ω₁(T)|=|Ω₁(C_R(A))|=p.
    By Lem 4.5, T,C_R(A) cyclic. T⊴R, R=TC_R(A) ⇒ R′⊆T.
```
